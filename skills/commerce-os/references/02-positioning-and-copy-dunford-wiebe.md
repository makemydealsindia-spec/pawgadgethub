# Pillars 2 & 3: Brand Positioning (April Dunford) & Conversion Copywriting (Joanna Wiebe)

Reference frameworks modeled on **April Dunford** ([Obviously Awesome Positioning Guide](https://www.aprildunford.com/post/a-quickstart-guide-to-positioning)) and **Joanna Wiebe** ([Copyhackers Conversion Copywriting](https://copyhackers.com/conversion-copywriting-defined/)).

## Pillar 2: April Dunford's 5-Component Positioning Canvas

Before writing a single headline, lock in the brand's positioning using [April Dunford's 5-step process](https://www.aprildunford.com/post/a-quickstart-guide-to-positioning) so the product is never judged as a generic commodity:

1. **Competitive Alternatives**: What would your best customers actually do or buy if your brand did not exist? (Include "doing nothing", legacy retail stores, or cheap mass-market alternatives, without disparaging named competitors).
2. **Unique Attributes**: What specific features, sourcing, formulation, craftsmanship, or operational capabilities do you have that the alternatives lack?
3. **Value (and Proof) for Customers**: Translate every unique attribute into a tangible benefit for the buyer, backed by concrete proof.
4. **Best-Fit Target Customer Characteristics**: Define the exact attitudes, triggers, and situational context of buyers who care deeply about that value right now.
5. **Market Category (Frame of Reference)**: Choose the category context that makes your unique value immediately obvious at a glance.

### Deliverable: The Positioning & Messaging Matrix

Always output a structured positioning table mapping:

- **Alternative Habit/Option** -> **Why It Falls Short** -> **Our Unique Mechanism** -> **Customer Proof Point**.

## Pillar 3: Joanna Wiebe's Conversion Copywriting System

Follow [Joanna Wiebe's Copyhackers methodology](https://copyhackers.com/conversion-copywriting-defined/): conversion copywriting moves a specific reader toward a specific action using evidence and customer language rather than clever slogans.

### 1. Voice-of-Customer (VoC) Message Mining

Extract exact phrases from customer reviews, support tickets, Reddit threads, and competitor 2-to-4 star reviews:

- **Trigger Events**: What happened in the buyer's life right before they started looking for this product?
- **Desired Outcomes**: What exact words do they use to describe their ideal result?
- **Anxieties & Hesitations**: What almost stopped them from buying, or what burned them in the past?

### 2. Matching Copy to the 5 Stages of Awareness

Match headline angle and page length to the visitor's awareness stage:

- **Unaware**: Lead with a relatable story, identity truth, or surprising symptom.
- **Problem-Aware**: Lead with the specific pain point or frustration and agitate the cost of ignoring it (PAS: Problem, Agitation, Solution).
- **Solution-Aware**: Lead with the desired outcome and introduce your **Unique Mechanism** (why your method or formulation works where standard options fail).
- **Product-Aware**: Lead with differentiators, social proof, comparison tables, and objection handling.
- **Most Aware**: Lead directly with the Grand Slam Offer, bundle value, guarantee, and CTA.

### 3. High-Converting Copy Rules (Zero-Fluff Checklist)

- Apply the **Rule of One** to every page or email: one reader, one big idea, one promise, and one clear call to action.
- Replace every vague adjective ("premium", "world-class", "revolutionary") with a concrete number, sensory detail, or verifiable mechanism.
- Put **Button Copy** in first-person value language ("Get My Starter Kit", "Claim 20% Bundle Savings") with **Click-Trigger Microcopy** right underneath ("Ships in 24 hours. 60-day risk-free guarantee.").
- Never use em-dashes or en-dashes in copy; use clean periods, commas, colons, or parentheses.
