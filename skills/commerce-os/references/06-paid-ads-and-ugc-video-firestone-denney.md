# Pillars 10 & 11: Paid Acquisition (Ezra Firestone) & Short-Form UGC Scripting (Dara Denney)

Reference frameworks modeled on **Ezra Firestone** ([Smart Paid Traffic System](https://smartmarketer.com/smart-paid-traffic/)) and **Dara Denney** ([Performance Creative & UGC Workflow](https://motionapp.com/event/creative-strategy-workflow-with-dara-denney/)).

## Pillar 10: Ezra Firestone's Smart Paid Traffic & Pre-Sell System

Follow [Ezra Firestone's e-commerce traffic system](https://smartmarketer.com/smart-paid-traffic/) to turn cold paid impressions on Meta, Google, and TikTok into profitable customers:

### 1. The 3-Tier Full-Funnel Campaign Architecture

- **Tier 1: Cold Prospecting & Pre-Sell (65% to 75% of Budget)**:
  - Do not send all cold social traffic straight to a raw checkout page. Route cold prospects through **Value-First Pre-Sell Advertorials** ("5 Reasons Why...", Founder Story article, or Interactive Buying Guide) or a dedicated Nik Sharma landing page.
  - Let broad/algorithmic targeting find buyers by using **Creative as Targeting**: each ad angle calls out a specific customer avatar and problem in the first 3 seconds.
- **Tier 2: Warm Engagement & Social Proof Retargeting (15% to 20% of Budget)**:
  - Retarget video viewers (50%+), social engagers, and advertorial readers with **Customer Testimonial Collages**, unboxing demonstrations, and "Overcoming Objections" ads.
- **Tier 3: Hot Cart/PDP Abandonment Boomerang (10% to 15% of Budget)**:
  - Retarget 7-to-14 day Product Viewers and Cart Abandoners with direct offer reminders, bundle savings, guarantee reinforcement, and founder FAQ videos.

### 2. The 6-Week Promotional Rhythm

Layer a structured **6-Week Promotional Calendar** over evergreen traffic (seasonal drop, bundle event, founder anniversary, limited gift-with-purchase, or educational challenge) to convert accumulated warm leads and email/SMS subscribers into cash-flow spikes without devaluing the core brand.

## Pillar 11: Dara Denney's Modular Performance Creative & UGC Scripting System

Apply [Dara Denney's performance creative workflow](https://motionapp.com/event/creative-strategy-workflow-with-dara-denney/) to script high-converting video ads and organic Reels/TikToks:

### 1. The Modular Hook-Body-CTA Scripting Architecture

Always write video scripts in interchangeable blocks so 1 body shoot produces 4 to 6 testable ad variations:

- **4 Visual + Verbal Hooks (0 to 3 Seconds)**:
  - *Hook A (Pattern Interrupt / Contrarian)*: Bold visual action + challenging a common myth in the niche.
  - *Hook B (Problem Callout / Symptom)*: Calling out the exact frustration the buyer felt this morning.
  - *Hook C (Social Proof / Viral Reaction)*: "I finally tested the [Product] everyone in [Niche] has been talking about..."
  - *Hook D (Founder / Behind-the-Scenes)*: "As someone who works in [Industry], here is what nobody tells you about [Category]..."
- **Core Body Module (3 to 22 Seconds)**:
  1. **Agitation & Failed Alternatives (3-7s)**: Why what they tried before didn't work.
  2. **Product Introduction & Unique Mechanism (7-14s)**: Show the product in hand/action within the first 5 seconds; explain the 1 mechanism that makes it different.
  3. **Tangible Proof & Sensory Payoff (14-22s)**: Macro close-up, real result, review overlay, or side-by-side comparison.
- **2 Call-to-Action (CTA) Endings (22 to 30 Seconds)**:
  - *CTA 1 (Offer + Risk Reversal)*: Highlight the bundle bonus and guarantee ("Tap below to get the starter bundle with our 60-day guarantee").
  - *CTA 2 (Scarcity / Direct Action)*: Highlight fast dispatch or limited batch availability.

### 2. Creative Iteration & Diagnostic Metrics

- **Low Thumbstop Rate (<25% 3-second views)**: Keep the body, swap the first 3 seconds (visual hook, opening line, and text overlay).
- **Low Hold Rate (<15% 15-second views)**: Tighten pacing, add faster B-roll cuts every 2 to 3 seconds, and move product demonstration earlier.
- **High Watch Time but Low Outbound CTR (<1%)**: Strengthen the offer angle and make the final 5-second CTA more specific.
